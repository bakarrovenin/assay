# Protected copy

These lines are fixed. They may be moved, re-set, or re-styled, but the words
and the sentence boundaries stay exactly as written here. Do not paraphrase,
merge, split, or run them on into a following clause.

They are load-bearing: each one is either the argument the site exists to make
or a commitment we have made in public. Rewriting one quietly changes what
Assay claims.

1. Your AI fixed it. We check whether it actually did.
2. A patch can pass every test and leave the hole wide open.
3. It fixed the test, not the bug.
4. Every vendor grades its own homework.
5. A test is a sample, not a specification.
6. If you let a student write their own exam, they will pass it. You test them
   on questions they have not seen.
7. The alert turning green and the bug being fixed are two different events.
   The industry measures the first one.
8. We have no customers yet. These are the terms we intend to hold to.
9. Take nobody's word for it.

## Notes

Line 5 is a whole sentence, ending in a full stop. A cut that continues it as
", and ..." is a change to a protected line even though every word survives.
That exact case came up in the design system v2 rebuild and was rejected.

Line 1 may be broken across lines for setting. The hard line breaks it once
carried were tuned for a smaller display size and are not part of the line.

Typographic apostrophes are the rendered form of line 9 and of any other line
carrying one. The apostrophe character is not the protected part.
